About Frontlines

The Mission

Frontlines is streetwear built to say something. Every hoodie, tee, hat, and beanie carries a message aimed straight at addiction — fentanyl, meth, and alcohol — and at the people fighting it, recovering from it, or standing beside someone who is. It's not quiet merch. It's meant to be seen and to start conversations.

About Ryan

Ryan Brusher has been personally impacted by addiction, and that experience is what drives Frontlines. He's in recovery himself and runs the Frontlines fentanyl support meeting, a space where people affected by fentanyl addiction find support and community. What started as a simple idea — matching shirts for him and his buddies at the meeting — turned into a whole movement.

For Ryan, this isn't just a brand. It's a cause he cares about deeply, born out of a fight he knows firsthand. Every piece of Frontlines gear carries the same message: recovery is possible, and nobody has to fight this alone.
